*The Proposal*, after Anton Chekhov, was my final project for the Principles of Directing course in my MA, supervised by Prof. Esmaeil Shafiee. The challenge was to keep strictly to the original text, with no additions or cuts, while bringing our own tone and style to the staging.

## Director's Note

We analysed Chekhov's text closely with the actors, researched commedia dell'arte, and brought its gestures into our interpretation. Working with a classical Russian text and with Chekhov was new for me.
