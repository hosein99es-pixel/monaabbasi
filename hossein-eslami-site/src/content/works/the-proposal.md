Chekhov's one-act comedy *The Proposal* was my final project for the Principles of Directing course in my MA, under the supervision of Prof. Esmaeil Shafiee. The task was to stage the text exactly as written, with no cuts or additions, while finding our own tone and style in the staging.

## Director's Note

We analysed the text closely with the actors and decided to bring in the physical vocabulary of commedia dell'arte. Researching commedia and fitting its gestures to a classical Russian text was new territory for me; the challenge was to use it without breaking Chekhov's play.
