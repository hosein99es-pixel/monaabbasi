*A Requiem for Oedipus* was a student production at Shahid Beheshti University, made with the university's theatre association and philosophy society and directed by Abtin Gholampour. I was a member of the chorus. After researching the chorus in Sophocles, we reduced it to four performers. It was my first production at university, and an early lesson in persistence and teamwork.
