*A Requiem for Oedipus* was a student production at Shahid Beheshti University, made with the university's theatre association and philosophy society and directed by Abtin Gholampour. I played one of the chorus members. After researching the chorus in Sophocles' plays, we reduced it to four performers. It was my first production at the university, and a lesson in perseverance and teamwork.
