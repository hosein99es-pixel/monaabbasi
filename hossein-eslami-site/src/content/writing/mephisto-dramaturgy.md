What is left of art once beauty starts working for those in control? The dramaturgical research for *Mephisto For Ever* moves between Goethe's *Faust*, its appropriation by Nazi cultural policy, and the life of Gustaf Gründgens. The aim is for Mephisto to appear not as a devil who comes from outside, but as something already living inside the artist.
